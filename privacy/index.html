On this site

This site’s fonts and images are served locally. Its pages contain no advertisements or third-party comment system.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand visits: pages viewed, traffic sources, and interactions. Your browser contacts Google and may store analytics cookies. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled in the site’s configuration.

How Google uses data ↗

External services

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, Piggy, and podcast platforms take you to services with their own privacy policies. The Buy or Rent calculator loads directly on the Projects page as the demo area approaches the viewport. It contacts its hosting provider and loads its own external resources, including jsDelivr and Google Analytics. The “View screenshot” button removes the live calculator and displays a local image. Without JavaScript or iframe support, only that screenshot appears. Videos embedded in older articles are shown as links: they do not contact their platform until you choose to open it.

Hosting

The site is designed for GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve the site. See its privacy statement for details.

Writing and translations

Historical articles retain their original text and publication dates. AI-generated summaries are labeled on their pages. Any future AI-generated translation will be a separate, clearly labeled page linking to the original.
