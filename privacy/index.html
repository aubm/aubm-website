A place to share ideas, not to follow you around.

On this site

This static site loads no analytics, advertisements, or third-party comment system. Fonts and images are served locally. The site sets no tracking cookies.

External services

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, Piggy, and podcast platforms take you to services with their own privacy policies. Videos embedded in older articles are shown as links: they do not contact their platform until you choose to open it.

Hosting

The site is designed for GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve the site. See its privacy statement for details.

Writing and translations

Historical articles retain their original text and publication dates. AI-generated summaries are labeled on their pages. Any future AI-generated translation will be a separate, clearly labeled page linking to the original.
